Our Story

Founded in 1993 by Al and Kathy Kelley, Kelley Accounting & Tax Service has been the cornerstone of financial expertise in Millbrook, Alabama for over three decades. What started as a small family business has grown into the community's most trusted accounting firm, built on the foundation of personal relationships and unwavering integrity.

From our office at 3348 Main Street, we've had the privilege of serving generations of Millbrook families and watching local businesses flourish under our guidance. Our commitment extends far beyond numbers – we're invested in the financial success and peace of mind of every client we serve.

32 Years of Service: Established in 1993, serving Millbrook families and businesses through every economic season with consistent, reliable expertise.
Family-Owned Values: As a family business, we understand the importance of trust, personal attention, and building lasting relationships with our clients.
IRS Authorized: Certified e-file provider with full IRS authorization, ensuring secure, accurate, and timely tax processing for all our clients.
